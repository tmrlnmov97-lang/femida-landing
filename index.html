What is the deadline for contesting a dismissal?

▤ Legal analysis

◈ femid.ai 3 sources

An employee can contest a dismissal in court within one month of receiving the dismissal order [1]. If the order was not handed over, the period runs from the day the employee learned of it [2]. Courts treat the deadline strictly but restore it for a valid reason, such as illness [3].
